CoreCompRx Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

CoreCompRx | 390 Lincoln Ave, Ste B3, Hatboro, PA 19040 | 445-300-7010

This Privacy Policy describes how CoreCompRx ("CoreCompRx," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information about patients and website visitors. It also explains our SMS/text messaging practices for patient communication, including address and patient information verification.

This policy is intended for website publication and SMS carrier registration. It is not a substitute for legal advice or for any separate HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices that CoreCompRx may be required to provide as a covered health care provider.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information generated through pharmacy services, and limited technical information when you use our website or communicate with us. The information we may collect includes:

  • Contact information: name, mailing address, delivery address, phone number, email address, and preferred communication method.
  • Patient and prescription information: date of birth, medication information, prescribing provider information, allergies or clinical notes you provide, refill information, insurance information, and other information needed to provide pharmacy services.
  • Verification information: information used to confirm your identity, address, contact details, prescription status, or delivery information.
  • Payment and billing information: payment status, billing details, insurance claims information, and related transaction records. We do not intentionally store full payment card numbers on our website unless handled by a secure payment processor.
  • SMS/text messaging information: your mobile phone number, opt-in status, message content, message delivery status, date/time of messages, and opt-out requests.
  • Website and technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring pages, and similar information collected through cookies or similar technologies when applicable.

2. How We Use Information

CoreCompRx uses personal information for pharmacy operations, patient service, compliance, and communication. We may use information to:

  • Provide pharmacy services, fill prescriptions, process refills, coordinate medication delivery or pickup, and communicate with prescribers, insurers, caregivers, and authorized representatives as appropriate.
  • Verify patient identity, address, delivery details, prescription information, insurance information, or other information needed to provide accurate pharmacy services.
  • Send service-related SMS/text messages, phone calls, emails, or other communications about prescription status, address verification, information verification, refill coordination, delivery coordination, appointment or care coordination, account questions, and customer service.
  • Respond to patient questions, requests, complaints, or support needs.
  • Process payments, billing, insurance claims, chargebacks, and related accounting records.
  • Comply with federal and state laws, pharmacy board requirements, HIPAA and other health privacy obligations, fraud prevention, security, auditing, and recordkeeping obligations.
  • Maintain and improve our website, services, security, and internal operations.

3. SMS/Text Messaging Program

By providing your mobile phone number and consenting to receive text messages from CoreCompRx, you agree that CoreCompRx may send SMS/text messages to the mobile number you provided. Messages may include patient address verification, patient information verification, prescription or refill status, delivery or pickup coordination, customer service communications, and other pharmacy service-related messages.

  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your pharmacy needs and interactions with CoreCompRx.
  • Message and data rates: Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Opt out: You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. After you send STOP, we may send one final confirmation message and then stop sending non-essential SMS messages to that number unless you opt in again.
  • Help: For help, reply HELP or call CoreCompRx at 445-300-7010.
  • No purchase requirement: Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
  • Alternative communication: If you opt out of SMS, we may contact you by phone, mail, portal, email, or another method where permitted and appropriate for pharmacy operations.

4. SMS Consent and Phone Number Privacy

CoreCompRx does not sell, rent, or share SMS opt-in consent, mobile phone numbers, or SMS/text messaging data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate our messaging program, such as telecommunications carriers, messaging platforms, and technology vendors, solely as needed to deliver messages, maintain security, keep records, and support our pharmacy operations. These service providers are not permitted to use SMS consent or mobile information for their own marketing.

We may also disclose information when required or permitted by law, including to comply with legal process, pharmacy regulations, HIPAA, fraud prevention, patient safety, audits, or law enforcement requests.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell patient personal information. We may disclose personal information only as needed for lawful business, pharmacy, health care, operational, and compliance purposes, including:

  • Health care and pharmacy operations: to prescribers, health care providers, insurance plans, pharmacy benefit managers, delivery providers, laboratories, caregivers, or authorized representatives when appropriate for treatment, payment, or health care operations.
  • Service providers: to vendors that support our pharmacy, billing, delivery, technology, website, messaging, security, records, or customer service functions.
  • Legal and regulatory purposes: to government agencies, courts, audits, pharmacy boards, law enforcement, or other parties when required or permitted by law.
  • Business transactions: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or transfer of assets, subject to applicable privacy and health information protections.
  • With your direction or authorization: when you ask us to share information or provide written authorization where required.

6. HIPAA and Protected Health Information

As a pharmacy, CoreCompRx may collect, use, and disclose protected health information (PHI). We handle PHI in accordance with applicable federal and state health privacy laws, including HIPAA when applicable. Uses and disclosures of PHI may include treatment, payment, health care operations, public health reporting, regulatory compliance, patient safety, and other uses or disclosures allowed or required by law.

Patients may have rights under HIPAA and applicable state law, which may include the right to request access to certain health information, request amendments, request restrictions, request confidential communications, receive an accounting of certain disclosures, and receive a copy of a Notice of Privacy Practices. To exercise privacy rights, contact CoreCompRx using the contact information below.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies to operate the website, improve performance, understand visitor activity, and protect security. You may be able to adjust cookie settings through your browser. Some website functions may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information and health information. No website, transmission method, or electronic storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send highly sensitive information by SMS unless requested and appropriate for pharmacy operations, because text messaging may not be fully secure depending on your device, carrier, and settings.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information and pharmacy records for as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal and regulatory requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable pharmacy, health care, tax, and business recordkeeping requirements.

  • You may update your contact information by contacting CoreCompRx.
  • You may request privacy-related assistance or exercise available rights by contacting CoreCompRx using the information below.
